ONNX_HEAP_GRAPH_COPY_HYPOTHESIS_V2 (frozen 2026-10-22 after C1 was measured and reverted)
Evidence: provider construction raises Java heap commitment 35 -> 244 MB in all three baseline children while
heap use settles at ~120 MB; G1 keeps that commitment for the whole run, and it adds to process RSS on top of
the native session and inference memory. The cause is reading each whole graph (216.6 MB, 115.8 MB) into a
heap byte[] and passing it to createSession(byte[]), whose JNI path makes a further native copy.
Hypothesis: loading each graph into an exact-size off-heap direct ByteBuffer snapshot, hashing that snapshot,
and passing it to createSession(ByteBuffer) removes the humongous heap allocations and the JNI copy, lowering
persistent heap commitment and the process peak, without changing identity checks, limits or outputs.
Candidate C2 (one change, internal, no API): ManagedTableSession reads the model through a FileChannel into
ByteBuffer.allocateDirect(size) after checking size against maxModelBytes, verifies SHA-256 over exactly those
bytes, and creates the session from that buffer. Arena settings are unchanged (C1 is not applied).
