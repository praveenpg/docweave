# Explicit overlaid digit padding

This development experiment appends one caller-selected native table policy to the
eight policies previously executed on each complete original three-page financial PDF.
The manifests were frozen before the new policy's first extraction and bind source,
reference, prior-report and candidate production-source hashes. Original references,
ordinal comparison, association and all failures remain unchanged. Raw publisher files
remain outside Git. These are two periods from one issuer, not independent broad acceptance.

`RULED_AND_SHADED_ROWS_COMPACT_PADDING` projects out a unique shifted ASCII space
overlapping two adjacent overlapping digit advances in numeric body cells only. It retains
original source membership, coordinates and preprojection character budgets. Duplicate or
ambiguous evidence, ordinary spaces, NBSP, actual gaps, labels and headers are preserved.
No digit is inferred and no reference value enters extraction. The earlier shaded policy
remains unchanged. See [usage and constraints](../../../docs/pdf-support.md#explicit-overlaid-digit-padding-unreleased).

The July document improves from 77/114 exact ordinal rows under the earlier shaded policy
to 114/114 under the new option: operations 40/40, balance sheet 37/37, cash flows 37/37.
The April document remains 110/113: 37/40, 37/37 and 36/36 respectively. Its three operations
text mismatches remain open. Neither document has missing or extra rows under the new option.
The original [frozen-code transfer](../pdf-shaded-business-transfer/README.md) stays intact.

Both full builds pass 1,085 tests with zero failures, errors or skips on actual Java21/25,
including strict public Javadocs. Strict private Javadocs and the pinned formatter also pass.
Both periods' paired reports are equal apart from runtime; all24 prior case/policy observations
per period remain unchanged, and all52 prior scientific observations remain unchanged on both
JDKs. Frozen production-source hashes were checked against the final source bytes. The explicit
online upstream check passes; its current release/main observations remain distinct from the
older measured reference runtime. No release, tag or Central publication occurs.

Reports, paired-runtime receipts and scientific regression evidence accompany this protocol.
Each report retains all 27 case/policy observations, including unsuccessful
older policies. These source-specific row-content results do not establish physical cell/span
truth, semantic figure fidelity, general OCR accuracy or overall PDF completion.
